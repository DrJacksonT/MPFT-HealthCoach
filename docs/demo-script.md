# Five-minute demonstration

## Set-up

Open the landing page and state: "This is a synthetic research prototype. It is not a clinical service and nobody monitors it."

## 0:00 to 0:40: purpose and boundaries

Show the headline and the two capability panels. Point out that the main route is a programme, not a blank chatbot. Mention local browser storage and the visible delete control.

## 0:40 to 1:20: synthetic onboarding

Load **Family focus**. Explain that it is fictional: 20 cigarettes a day, broad 40s age band, high blood pressure, family and health motivations. Open the review briefly. Point out that pack-years and spending are ordinary code calculations, not model output or personal risk scores.

## 1:20 to 2:20: personalised evidence translation

Open the evidence page. Explain that broad profile tags rank a small eligible library. Open one card at each depth:

1. the plain English finding;
2. the number, population and timeframe where available;
3. limitations and the explicit statement that population evidence is not an exact prediction;
4. the original source link.

Open the admin dashboard in a separate tab and match the card ID to its status, source, last check, review date and verification note.

## 2:20 to 3:10: goal

Open **My plan** and choose **Plan for one trigger**. Emphasise that the person chooses the goal and can select learning without being pushed to quit immediately.

## 3:10 to 3:45: check-in and progress

Add a check-in below the fictional baseline. Show cigarettes avoided and money not spent as labelled estimates. Show the table below the chart. Add a higher day if useful and point out the non-judgemental response: the day remains part of the record and does not reset progress.

## 3:45 to 4:30: grounded coach and safety boundary

Ask: "I keep smoking when work gets stressful. What could I plan?" Show the bounded response and citation IDs. Then ask: "I have severe chest pain. Is this serious?" Show that the deterministic boundary says the tool cannot assess symptoms, is not monitored, and directs urgent help appropriately. It must not claim that anybody was alerted.

## 4:30 to 5:00: provenance and deletion

Return to **Help & data**. Show the trusted NHS and MPFT public resources. Select **Delete my demo data** and confirm return to the landing page. Close by stating that the research question concerns comprehension, motivation and behaviour, not whether a chatbot can sound convincing.
