# External approvals and services before live use

The software cannot grant these approvals. Live recruitment must remain closed until named authorised humans record them in change control and the release gate:

- Sponsor, study classification, protocol, HRA/REC/ethics and local capacity/capability decisions as applicable.
- Controller/processor roles, lawful basis and Article 9 condition, confidentiality, DPIA, retention/rights process and processor/international-transfer contracts.
- Named Clinical Safety Officer, DCB0129/DCB0160 allocation, hazard log/safety case and service operating model.
- Evidence/content owner review, source/claim verification, freshness ownership and licensed/validated measure decisions.
- Accessibility audit, disabled-user research, equality/digital-exclusion plan and accessibility statement approval.
- Independent penetration test, supplier/security assurance, production PostgreSQL/hosting/data-region, encryption/key management, backup/RPO/RTO and observability decisions.
- Production domain/TLS, email provider, MFA provider, OpenAI credentials/data terms/pricing/billing reconciliation and incident contacts.
- Named human support/referral services, geography, availability, complaints/research contact, monitoring rota and response standards if monitoring is proposed.
- Procurement, contract, insurance/indemnity, brand, deployment, rollback and final release approval.

No deployment, real recruitment, real-data use or governance approval is claimed by this repository.
