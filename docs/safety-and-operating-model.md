# Safety and operating model

Deterministic routing runs before AI and recognises emergency danger, self-harm, severe distress, concerning physical symptoms, medication/treatment questions, safeguarding/coercion, acute gambling financial harm, prohibited gambling advice, routine support and out-of-scope requests. Generated output is checked again.

Participants receive immediate self-directed routes to configured services. The site never says a clinician has been alerted or that help is coming. Staff safety review is a retrospective authorised quality workflow, not an emergency inbox or clinical command centre. No staffed monitoring rota is configured.

Smoking tools are compassionate and non-diagnostic. Medication choices are referred to a GP, pharmacist or stop-smoking professional. Gambling simulation offers only pause, block/self-exclusion, money-access separation and human support; it never offers odds, chasing, bypass, borrowing, debt products or financial advice.

Before live use, named clinical-safety ownership, DCB0129/DCB0160 responsibilities, hazard log acceptance, local services/geography, availability checks, escalation wording, complaints routes and any response-time operating model require human approval.
